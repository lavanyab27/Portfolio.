01 · Overview

Project Brief

Bournvita has strong brand equity among millennials and older generations, but Gen Z views it as a childhood relic. Rebrand the product in such a way that Gen Z connects to the product and it is seen as a product used by all.

02 · Research

Understanding Gen Z

Challenges

Swipe to explore challenges →

Perception Gap

Many Gen Z consumers view Bournvita as a 'child's drink'. We needed to retain brand trust while signaling grown-up relevance.

Health Expectations

Gen Z expects transparency on sugar, calories, and functional benefits. Packaging must communicate nutrition clearly and credibly.

Standout on Shelf & Social

Design must perform both in physical retail and in short-form content — it has to stop the scroll and invite an immediate interaction.

Strategy & Concept

Swipe to explore strategy →
01

Youthful Rituals

Frame Bournvita as a ritual for energy and creativity — quick mixes, recipe moments, and morning routines that can be shared online.

02

Social-First Packaging

Introduce formats designed for sharing: vibrant sachet and limited edition artist-collab sleeves that encourage UGC.

03

Functional Nutrition

Low-sugar options, protein-enhanced blends, and clear callouts for vitamins—packaging communicates benefits instantly with simple icons and short copy.

04

Sustainable & Affordable

Eco-friendly materials, reduced secondary packaging, and price tiers that allow trial without commitment.

"Bournvita for Gen Z — fuel your focus, share your moments, and make the ritual yours."
