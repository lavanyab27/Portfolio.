Visual System & Strategy

How the three formats stay cohesive while serving distinct campaign goals

01 • Color Rhythm

High-Contrast Feed Stopping Power

Alternating warm cream, deep espresso, and crisp powder blue backgrounds prevents scroll fatigue across multi-slide decks while keeping event times legible on mobile screens.

#3D1D1C #FFEDCA #9DB4C4 #7F2834
02 • Typographic Pairing

Display Hook + Editorial Detail

Bold uppercase display headers anchor Slide 1 at thumbnail scale in the profile grid, while high-contrast serif titles and clean sans-serif metadata organize schedules inside.

Syne Bold Playfair Display Poppins
03 • Pacing & Length

Right-Sized Per Campaign Goal

Each format uses the exact slide count needed: 2 slides for quick schedule saves, 1 poster frame for immediate event impact, and 3 slides for interactive mood matching.

4:5 Safe Zones Visual Continuity
